Asaan Finance by NRSP

Delete your Asaan Finance account

This page explains how to delete your account in the Asaan Finance app by the National Rural Support Programme (NRSP), which data is deleted, and which records NRSP must keep.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Asaan Finance and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password, tick "I understand that my account will be deleted permanently" and tap Delete.

You are logged out right away.

Option 2: Without the app

If you cannot open the app, send a request from your registered mobile number or by email:

Email: mer_dojlm@yahoo.com — subject "Account Deletion Request"
WhatsApp: +92 302 8582454

Please include your full name, CNIC number and registered mobile number so we can confirm the account is yours. We never ask for your password or OTP.

We confirm your identity and complete the deletion within 14 business days.

What is deleted

  • Your app login (CNIC and password) and profile: name, phone number, date of birth and profile photo.
  • Notification tokens, app notifications and your feedback messages.
  • If you have not received financing: your financing applications and the CNIC pictures you uploaded.

What NRSP keeps, and for how long

  • If you have received financing from NRSP, your application, identity (KYC) documents and repayment records are kept for as long as Pakistani law and our regulators require for financial records. After that they are deleted or anonymised.
  • Deleting the app account does not cancel financing you already have. Your installments continue and can be paid through the banks and agents shown in the app or at your NRSP branch.
