Delete your account

How to delete your Asaan Finance account, what is deleted and what NRSP must keep.

Option 1: In the app

The fastest way. You are logged out right away.

  1. 1
    Open Asaan Finance and sign in.
  2. 2
    Tap Settings at the bottom of the screen.
  3. 3
    Tap Delete Account.
  4. 4
    Enter your password, tick "I understand that my account will be deleted permanently" and tap Delete.

Option 2: Without the app

Send us your full name, CNIC number and registered mobile number. We confirm the account is yours and delete it within 14 business days.

WhatsApp Email

WhatsApp: +92 302 8582454 · Email: mer_dojlm@yahoo.com
We never ask for your password or OTP.

What is deleted

  • Your app login (CNIC and password) and profile: name, phone number, date of birth and profile photo.
  • Notification tokens, app notifications and your feedback messages.
  • If you have not received financing: your financing applications and the CNIC pictures you uploaded.

What NRSP keeps

If you have received financing from NRSP, your application, identity (KYC) documents and repayment records are kept for as long as Pakistani law and our regulators require for financial records. After that they are deleted or anonymised.

Deleting the app account does not cancel financing you already have. Your installments continue and can be paid through the banks and agents shown in the app or at your NRSP branch.
